# Erdős #77 — Ramsey asymptotics theorem and obstruction bank

Public release of the audited session ledger around the exponential growth of diagonal Ramsey numbers.

## Claim boundary

**The underlying Erdős problem is not closed.** The release separates proved lemmas and algebraic constructions from conditional composition hypotheses, diagnostics, external literature inputs, and explicit open obligations.

The central proved reductions include:

- thin-corridor comparison `r(n-a,n) <= R(n) <= 4^a r(n-a,n)`;
- equivalence of diagonal exponential growth with any sublinear off-diagonal corridor;
- generalized-inverse formulation through the minimum forced homogeneous-set function;
- clone saturation in critical Ramsey colourings;
- exact accumulation of summably sublinear composition error;
- a family of binary bilinear / polarity digraph constructions;
- exact pattern-collision and tensor-flag rank identities;
- a sharp fiber obstruction showing why the unrestricted mixed Cartesian polarity product cannot preserve the desired exponential vertex density while killing large independent sets;
- a perfect-matching reduction to fiber-free polarity digraphs.

The unresolved spine is a composition theorem strong enough to produce approximate supermultiplicativity or an equivalent container/rank bound without the fiber obstruction.
