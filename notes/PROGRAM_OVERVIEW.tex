\title{A Research Program for Erd\H{o}s--Graham Problem \#203:\\
Algebraic Structure, Conditional Routes, and the High-$\ell$ Analytic Seam}
\author{Jared Wilder}
\date{2026-05-13}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This note is the index and roadmap for a sequence of sixteen companion manuscripts on the Erd\H{o}s--Graham sequence \(m2^k3^\ell+1\).  It records what is proved unconditionally, what is conditional on named analytic inputs, and what remains open.  No assertion in this overview claims to resolve Erd\H{o}s--Graham Problem \#203.  The purpose of the program is to separate exact finite algebra and finite probability from the high-\(\ell\) analytic seam.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem}

For \(m\in\Z_{>0}\) with \((m,6)=1\), set
\[
A(m)=\bigl\{(k,\ell)\in\Z_{\ge0}^{\,2}:\ m2^k3^\ell+1\in\mathbb P\bigr\}.
\]
Erd\H{o}s and Graham asked whether \(|A(m)|=\infty\) for every such \(m\).  This program studies the question by isolating the exact local subgroup obstruction at modulus \(d\),
\[
\Gamma_d=\langle 2,3\rangle\subset(\Z/d\Z)^*,
\]
and reducing the global question to analytic distribution problems for the associated Kummer characters.

\section{Manuscript map}

The program consists of sixteen manuscripts, ordered logically below.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.5em,label=\textbf{[\arabic*]}]
\item \emph{A Subgroup Obstruction Calculus for Two-Generator Exponential Congruences} (Paper~1).  Elementary finite-group framework.  No analytic input.
\item \emph{CRT Synchronization and Projective Kummer Slopes in Two-Generator Subgroups} (Paper~2).  Squarefree-modulus structure of \(\Gamma_d\), rank-defect criterion, projective Kummer slope \(\sigma_\ell(q)\), the collision graph \(G_\ell(Q)\), and collision excess \(\mathcal C_\ell(Q)\).
\item \emph{Exact \(\Gamma\)-Fiber Local Density and the Kummer-Character Remainder} (technical note).  Exact identity \(|B_q(c)|=\gcd(a_q,b_q)\,\1_{\Gamma_q}(c)\), closed form for \(\Delta_m(q)\), Pappalardi-controlled pointwise bound, and small-prime verification.
\item \emph{A Jet-Primitive Stepanov Auxiliary Theorem for the \((2,3)\)-Orbit} (technical note).  Stepanov-method orbit bound outside the Pappalardi exceptional set; retained as a transferable method even where the exact fiber identity supersedes it.
\item \emph{A Conditional Density-Zero Kummer Obstruction Theorem for Erd\H{o}s--Graham Problem \#203} (Paper~3).  Density-zero exceptional set for cumulative Kummer obstruction energy, conditional on a Pappalardi-type subgroup-order lower bound.
\item \emph{A Kummer Distribution Criterion for Erd\H{o}s--Graham Problem \#203} (Paper~4).  Criterion paper isolating weighted PHNC, Bernoulli KRWS, KRC, KFVT, Kummer-BV, and BVK as named analytic inputs.
\item \emph{On Some Tempting but Insufficient Routes to Fixed-Radical Kummer Non-Concentration} (Paper~5).  Defensive publication documenting five routes that do not by themselves prove PHNC in the high-\(\ell\) seam.
\item \emph{Rank-r Synchronization Defects and Projective Kummer Geometry} (Paper~6).  Rank-\(r\) squarefree membership, synchronization-defect quotient, projective Kummer points in \(\mathbb P^{r-1}(\F_\ell)\), hyperplane atoms, rank-\(r\) star fields, prime-power lifting, and general-modulus factorization.
\item \emph{Exact Distribution, Matroid, and Lifting Addenda for the EG203 Obstruction Calculus} (Paper~7).  CRT independence, Poisson-binomial obstruction counts, Erdos-Kac criterion, Kummer log matroid, generalized Wieferich lifting, SSDP as congruence-lattice SVP, collision excess as finite Fourier energy, and Tate-module finite-linear port.
\item \emph{Kummer-Fourier Collision Variance and Weighted Hyperplane Matroid Sums} (Paper~8).  Corrects a false linear product formula and proves the exact squared-modulus Plancherel/CRT collision-variance identity for Bernoulli-Kummer obstruction vectors.
\item \emph{Kummer Cut-Gap Bounds for Fourier Collision Excess} (Paper~9).  Sharpens the Paper~8 identity into finite bounds controlled by the minimum Kummer cut \(\Lambda_*\), minimizer multiplicity \(k_*\), and spectral gap \(\Delta\), with a safe binary row-space/cocircuit specialization and row-balance decay certificate.
\item \emph{Kummer Row-Balance and the Chebotarev--Matroid Link} (Paper~10).  Kills rank-only row-balance, proves fixed-\(\ell\) Chebotarev row-balance for independent Kummer bases under a density-floor assumption, and isolates high-\(\ell\) angular non-concentration as the analytic seam.
\item \emph{From Prime Hecke Non-Concentration to Angular Kummer Collision Decay} (Paper~11).  Proves that ordinary PHNC, with a density floor or weighted version, implies the angular-energy lower bounds needed for Paper~9 after the sharp \(\ell^{-2}\) cosine loss.
\item \emph{Kummer-Fourier Smoothing and the Random-Walk Bridge} (Paper~12).  Proves Bernoulli-Kummer \(L^2\), total-variation, and collision-probability smoothing from weighted PHNC, and proves that deterministic linear-PHNC smoothing fails without affine non-concentration.
\item \emph{The Diagonal Barrier in Kummer Large-Sieve Transfers} (Paper~13).  Proves the exact vector-fiber Plancherel identity for weighted Kummer character sums, kills arbitrary-coefficient RTHLS as stated, and isolates KFVT as the correct sieve-transfer input.
\item \emph{Bad-Character Sparsity and the High-\(\ell\) Kummer Non-Concentration Barrier} (Paper~14).  Proves that a PHNC failure forces an entire cyclic line of \(\ell-1\) medium-bad characters, gives the exact mean-square/vector-fiber criterion, and promotes Kummer-sparsity as the missing analytic theorem rather than a BFI consequence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What is conditional}

The following statements are stated as theorems contingent on named hypotheses:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item (Paper 3) Density-zero Kummer obstruction theorem, conditional on a Pappalardi-type lower bound for subgroup order, with summable exceptional set.
\item (Paper 4) Conditional Erd\H{o}s--Graham \#203 criterion, conditional on weighted PHNC, KFVT, Kummer-BV, and BVK.
\item (Papers 12--13) The Bernoulli KRWS component is finite once weighted PHNC is supplied.  Arbitrary-coefficient RTHLS is replaced by the fiber-variance transfer input.
\item (Paper 4) GRH-conditional variant, conditional on uniform Kummer-GRH replacing PHNC.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What is open}

The primary named open non-concentration problem of the program is:

\begin{problem}[High-\(\ell\) prime Hecke non-concentration]
For every \(B>0\), prove the existence of \(C=C(B)>0\) such that, uniformly for all primes \(\ell\le(\log Q)^B\) and all nonzero \((r,s)\in\F_\ell^2\),
\[
\#\bigl\{q\le Q:q\equiv1\pmod\ell,\ 2^r3^s\in(\F_q^*)^\ell\bigr\}\le(1-\ell^{-C})\,\pi(Q;\ell,1).
\]
\end{problem}

Fixed-\(\ell\) Chebotarev gives eventual Kummer equidistribution.  Any effective growing-\(\ell\) subrange must be read from a precise effective Chebotarev theorem together with the discriminant/conductor bounds for the Kummer fields involved.  The seam \((\log Q)^{1-\varepsilon}<\ell\le(\log Q)^B\) remains open.  KFVT, Kummer-BV, and BVK also remain conditional sieve-transfer inputs.  Paper~5 documents routes that do not close PHNC.  Papers~6--14 expose additional exact algebraic, probabilistic, Fourier, and combinatorial structure around the seam, but do not claim an unconditional PHNC theorem.

\section{Honest status of Erd\H{o}s--Graham Problem \#203}

This program does not claim to resolve Problem \#203.  What is contributed:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item An elementary, exact algebraic framework for the local obstruction at squarefree and general odd prime-power moduli.
\item A precise identification of the high-\(\ell\) analytic seam that would supply the conditional implication to a negative resolution of \#203.
\item A defensive audit of methodological routes that do not close the seam.
\item Exact finite probability upgrades: CRT independence, Poisson-binomial laws, and a clean Erdos-Kac criterion.
\item Rank-\(r\), matroid, lifting, Fourier-energy, and Tate-module extensions that create reusable machinery beyond the original two-generator setting.
\item A corrected finite Fourier collision-variance identity replacing a false linear product formula with the exact squared-modulus CRT/Plancherel product.
\item A finite cut-gap compression of the collision spectrum through \((\Lambda_*,k_*,\Delta)\), with exact binary row-space/cocircuit specialization and row-balance decay certificates.
\item Fixed-\(\ell\) Chebotarev row-balance, plus conditional high-\(\ell\) angular non-concentration.
\item A PHNC-to-angular bridge showing ordinary PHNC is enough for collision decay after an \(\ell^{-2}\) cosine loss.
\item A Bernoulli-Kummer smoothing theorem closing the finite \(L^2\) KRWS seam under weighted PHNC, plus an affine counterexample explaining why deterministic smoothing requires affine non-concentration.
\item An exact Kummer large-sieve identity exposing the diagonal barrier and replacing arbitrary-coefficient RTHLS with vector-fiber variance transfer.
\item A bad-character sparsity criterion proving that a PHNC failure creates an \(\ell-1\)-character cyclic line, plus the exact mean-square/vector-fiber barrier for this route.
\item Numerical and computational helpers for small finite examples.
\end{itemize}

The high-\(\ell\) seam is the precise place where analytic work remains.
