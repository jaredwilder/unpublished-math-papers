# Validated Mathematics — audited long-tail theorem ledger

**Author:** Jared Wilder  
**Recovered source:** September 2026 validation audit  
**Public extraction:** 2026-09-11

This ledger collects proof-level assets that survived the audit. Status labels are literal. Several results also have focused subject packets elsewhere in this repository or formal counterparts in `jaredwilder/erdos-theorems`.

Two historical corrections are applied rather than silently copied: the #456 equality family is stated for **odd** primes because `p=2` is an endpoint exception under the campaign definitions, and the old `C(13,6,3)` degree-8 package is marked superseded by the later degree-9 theorem.

## #276 — common divisors of Fibonacci/Lucas-type recurrences

**Status:** `KERNEL_CHECKED`  
**Scope:** universal

For every sequence `a:N→N` satisfying

\[
a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_n,
\]

an integer `d` divides every term iff

\[
\boxed{d\mid\gcd(a_0,a_1).}
\]

Forward is immediate from `n=0,1`; reverse follows by two-step induction and closure of divisibility under addition. The recovered kernel receipt reports only the ordinary Mathlib axiom footprint used by the formal environment.

## Sidon-8 — no five-element Sidon subset of `Fin 8`

**Status:** `KERNEL_CHECKED`  
**Scope:** finite universal

Every five-element subset of `Fin 8` has a nontrivial equal pair-sum. Together with a four-element witness, this certifies the campaign finite optimum

\[
\boxed{f(8)=4}
\]

under its frozen Sidon convention. The upper half was kernel-exhausted with `decide` over all five-subsets.

## #400 — factorial-divisibility logarithmic bound

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal upper bound + infinite lower family

For fixed `k>=2`,

\[
\boxed{g_k(n)\le k(\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1),}
\]

hence

\[
g_k(n)=O_k(\log n),\qquad \sum_{n\le x}g_k(n)=O_k(x\log x).
\]

The proof uses Legendre at 2: if `∏a_i! | n!`, then

\[
\sum_i(a_i-s_2(a_i))\le n-s_2(n),
\]

and each binary digit sum is bounded by `floor(log2 n)+1`.

There is also the infinite lower family

\[
\boxed{g_k(m!)\ge m+k-3}
\]

from the tuple `(m!-1,m,1,...,1)`, since

\[
(m!-1)!\,m!=m!\, (m!-1)!.
\]

## #1142 — multiplicative-order modular sieve

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal sieve lemma

Let `Good(n)` mean `n-2^j` is prime whenever `1<2^j<n`. For odd prime `p`, `r=ord_p(2)`, if

\[
n>p+2^r
\]

and `n mod p` lies in the subgroup generated by 2, then `Good(n)` is impossible. In particular

\[
Good(n),\ n>21\Longrightarrow15\mid n.
\]

A focused proof packet is public at `../erdos1142-order-sieve/`.

## #1052 — two-prime-factor unitary-perfect classification

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal restricted classification

No odd unitary-perfect integer `n>1` exists, and among unitary-perfect integers with at most two distinct prime factors, the only one is

\[
\boxed{6}.
\]

For odd `n`,

\[
\sigma^*(n)=\prod_{p^a\parallel n}(p^a+1)
\]

has too large a 2-adic factor to equal `2n`. For `n=2^a q^b`, the equation rearranges to

\[
q^b(2^a-1)=2^a+1,
\]

so `2^a-1|2`, forcing `a=1` and then `q^b=3`.

## #247 — sparse-position irrationality

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** irrationality, not transcendence

If `a_n` is strictly increasing and

\[
\limsup a_n/n=\infty,
\]

then `Σ2^{-a_n}` is irrational. The release-day subject packet strengthens this to every integer base `b>=2`; see `../erdos247-sparse-binary-irrationality/`.

## #826 — divisor-bound tail elimination

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal reduction

For

\[
k>\sqrt n,
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{\tau(n+k)<3k.}
\]

Indeed `τ(m)<=2√m`, while `n<k^2` gives

\[
n+k<k^2+k\le2k^2,
\]

so

\[
\tau(n+k)<2\sqrt2\,k<3k.
\]

Therefore any bound `τ(n+k)<=Ck` proved on the hard range `k<=sqrt n` extends globally with constant `max(C,3)`.

## #1061 — no diagonal sigma solutions

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal structural lemma

There is no positive integer `a` satisfying

\[
\sigma(a)+\sigma(a)=\sigma(2a).
\]

Write `a=2^e m` with `m` odd. Multiplicativity gives

\[
\frac{\sigma(2a)}{\sigma(a)}
=
\frac{2^{e+2}-1}{2^{e+1}-1}>2.
\]

Hence all solutions of

\[
\sigma(a)+\sigma(b)=\sigma(a+b)
\]

are off-diagonal. Under ordered/unordered counting conventions, solutions therefore pair by swapping coordinates. The stronger aliquot-square construction is public at `../erdos1061-aliquot-square/`.

## #1073 — factorial-plus-one divisor shape

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal shape theorem only

If `u>1` and

\[
u\mid n!+1,
\]

then every prime `p|u` satisfies

\[
\boxed{p>n.}
\]

If `u` is composite, then

\[
\boxed{u>n^2.}
\]

The first statement follows because a prime `p<=n` would divide both `n!` and `n!+1`. A composite `u` contains at least two prime factors with multiplicity, each exceeding `n`.

This theorem does **not** imply the old campaign's false Wilson-to-prime-powers extension or the claimed `O(sqrt x)` count.

## #936 — powerful-number square-cube congruence restriction

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal structural restriction

Every odd powerful number can be written

\[
m=a^2b^3
\]

with `b` squarefree. For `n>=3`, if `2^n-1` is powerful then

\[
\boxed{b\equiv7\pmod8,}
\]

and if `2^n+1` is powerful then

\[
\boxed{b\equiv1\pmod8.}
\]

Collect each prime exponent `e>=2` as `e=2q+3r` with `r∈{0,1}`; the `r=1` primes form squarefree `b`. Since odd `a` has `a^2≡1 mod8` and odd `b` has `b^3≡b mod8`, one gets `b≡m mod8`.

## #477 — square-value tiling obstruction

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** exact square-value slice

For

\[
B=\{k^2:k\ge0\},
\]

there is no finite `A⊂Z` with unique representation

\[
\mathbb Z=A+B.
\]

The proof via differences of squares and the mod-4 obstruction is given in `../validated-new-gold-ledger/`.

## #700 — exact semiprime binomial-gcd formula

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal semiprime family

For primes `p<=q` and `n=pq`, with

\[
f(n)=\min_{1<k\le n/2}\gcd\left(n,{n\choose k}\right),
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{f(pq)=p.}
\]

The full proof and regression values are in `../validated-new-gold-ledger/`.

## #479 — infinite family for `n | 2^n-k`

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** infinite parametric family

For `j>=0` and every odd prime `p`,

\[
k=2^{2^j},\qquad n=2^jp
\]

satisfy

\[
\boxed{2^n\equiv k\pmod n.}
\]

Thus every such `k` has infinitely many witnesses.

## #885 — factor-difference square criterion

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** exact equivalence

For `N>=1,d>=0`,

\[
d=|a-b|\text{ for some }ab=N
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\boxed{d^2+4N\text{ is a square}.}
\]

The full-problem LCM construction in the historical ledger was false; this equivalence survives independently.

## #456 — prime-index equality family

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** infinite exact family, endpoint-corrected

For every **odd** prime `p`, at `n=p-1` the campaign quantities satisfy

\[
\boxed{m_n=p_n=p.}
\]

The `p=2` endpoint is excluded: under the campaign's positive-integer convention `m_1=1` while `p_1=2`.

## #289 — unique maximal 2-adic denominator barrier

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal parity obstruction

Every reciprocal sum over a nontrivial consecutive integer interval is nonintegral. More generally, in an integral sum of interval-block reciprocal sums, the number of blocks attaining the global maximal denominator `v_2` must be even.

The proof clears the global lcm and uses the unique maximal-`v_2` denominator in each consecutive interval.

## #243 — eventual divisibility irrationality barrier

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal conditional irrationality theorem

If eventually

\[
a_n\mid a_{n+1},\qquad a_{n+1}/a_n\to\infty,
\]

then `Σ1/a_n` is irrational. A focused proof packet is public at `../erdos243-divisibility-irrationality/`.

## #985 — 3 primitive modulo Fermat-prime subfamily

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** restricted conditional prime family

For every Fermat prime `p>=5`,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}_p(3)=p-1.}
\]

Indeed `p≡5 mod12`, so `(3/p)=-1`; Euler's criterion gives `3^{(p-1)/2}≡-1 mod p`. Since `p-1` is a power of 2, the order must be the full `p-1`.

## #274 — infinite-group reduction for distinct-cardinality exact coset covers

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED_LEAN_READY`  
**Scope:** universal cardinal reduction

Any nontrivial finite exact coset cover with pairwise-distinct part cardinalities cannot live in an infinite group; any greater-than-one-part counterexample must be finite. The cardinality proof is given in `../validated-new-gold-ledger/`.

## `C(13,6,3)` — historical degree-8 package, now superseded

The audited theorem that every pair has multiplicity at least 3, every point degree at least 8, and total degree excess above 8 equals 16 is correct. It is **not** the current strongest public statement.

The later exact use of `C(12,5,2)=9` upgrades every point degree to at least 9 and leaves only three possible global degree multisets. Current authority: `jaredwilder/combinatorial-records/covering-designs/C13-6-3/`.

## #1085 — one-dimensional exact unit-distance extremum

**Status:** `MATHEMATICALLY_AUDITED`  
**Scope:** exact `d=1` subcase

For `n>=1`,

\[
\boxed{f_1(n)=n-1.}
\]

The unit-distance graph of finitely many points on the line is a forest/path union and has at most `n-1` edges; an arithmetic progression with spacing 1 attains the bound.
