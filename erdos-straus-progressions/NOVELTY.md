This is deliberately a qualified historical claim, not a mathematical theorem. Such a collision would change the novelty label, not the validity of the parameterization itself.

## Exact statement searched

A complete if-and-only-if parameterization of every positive-integer solution

\[
\frac4n=\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z
\]

whose strictly increasing denominators form an arithmetic progression, including:

- the coprime `(a,d)` normal form;
- the parity split through `D=3a^2-d^2`;
- the minimal scale in each parity class;
- uniqueness and parameter recovery;
- the corollary that no such denominator triple is primitive.

## Closest located literature

1. Christian Elsholtz and Terence Tao, *Counting the number of solutions to the Erdős–Straus equation on unit fractions*, Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society 94 (2013), arXiv:1107.1010, DOI 10.1017/S1446788712000468. It studies counts and distributions of general Erdős–Straus solutions, not the exact AP-denominator classification here.

2. Marilena Jianu and Sever Angel Popescu, *On a problem related to Egyptian fractions*, Bulletin Mathématique de la Société des Sciences Mathématiques de Roumanie 64 (112), no. 4 (2021), 401–411. It treats integrality questions involving denominators in arithmetic progression in a broader Egyptian-fraction setting, but not this three-term `4/n` if-and-only-if parity parameterization.

3. Broader divisor, Type I/II and polynomial-family Erdős–Straus parameterizations. These cover different structural subclasses or solution-generating families; no equivalent of the exact AP classification, uniqueness statement and nonprimitive corollary was located in the search.
